Explained

Where this goes: why one AI company's revenue can look $20bn bigger or smaller depending on how you count.

Try first: If a café sells your lemonade and keeps part of each sale, is the full price your revenue?

The idea: A run rate takes one recent month of sales and multiplies by twelve. The harder question is who counts a sale. Imagine a café keeps 20 cents of a $1 lemonade: you can report $1 of revenue plus a 20-cent cost, or just the 80 cents you receive. Accountants call this gross versus net reporting. Reports say Anthropic counts sales through cloud partners gross and OpenAI does not, so figures built on one method made OpenAI look bigger than its own roughly $50bn run rate.

Still unknown: how much of the $20bn gap is accounting and how much is investor extrapolation. Neither company has published the split.
